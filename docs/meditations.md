## Mindfulness and Breathing

"Mindfulness" is the practiced art of living in the present moment. One way to focus on the here-and-now is to take a moment - literally a minute or so - to still the body, quiet the mind, and focus on breathing.

This might not come easy. For most of us, lots of worries flit into our heads the moment we sit still. That's okay! For the next minute or so, let the thoughts flutter right past. Over time, you'll get better at quieting your thoughts. Blossom can help! Stare at the colorful lights while they pulse, flicker, and change...

There's a breathing technique called "Box Breathing." It's easy to remember: Inhale for four seconds, hold for four seconds, exhale for four seconds, and hold for four seconds. Breathe deeply! You should see your stomach rising as well as your chest. Blossom can help here, too: just follow along with the lights!

## Blossom's Meditation Mode

When you enter meditation mode, Blossom will ask how long you want to peace out. It'll guide you through timed or open-ended sessions. When a session starts, you can follow the on-screen instructions or (much better) put your phone away and just focus on the Blossom.

>[!TIP]
>If you run Meditations on Blossom in "Offline" mode, you won't get interrupted by Internet notifications. It's a feature!

Simply follow the guidance of the white lights: They'll fill up during the four seconds you inhale, remain lit while you hold your breath, and they'll empty during your exhale. Imagine white light filling and emptying from your lungs. Focus on the colors as they change between breaths.

Five gentle white pulses and a return to the ambient animation mode will let you know your meditation is complete. Even if you don't feel better or more present... at least you spent the last minute or so looking at something nice.
